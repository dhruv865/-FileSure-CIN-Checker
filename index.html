Understanding company identification numbers

A Corporate Identity Number (CIN) is a unique identification number assigned to companies registered in India.

A Limited Liability Partnership Identification Number (LLPIN) identifies a registered Limited Liability Partnership.

What does FileSure check?

  • CIN length and character structure.
  • Recognized state codes.
  • Incorporation year format and supported range.
  • Company type code structure.
  • LLPIN structure according to the current validation rules.
  • Duplicate entries within your submission.

Important verification note

FileSure performs structural validation only. A valid-format result does not confirm that a company or LLP exists, is active, or is registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA). Consult official MCA records for confirmation.
